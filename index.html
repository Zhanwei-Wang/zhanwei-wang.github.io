From Edge to Space.

Communication, Computing and AI

Hi, I’m Zhanwei Wang.

I work at the intersection of wireless networks and artificial intelligence—connecting distributed computing, learning, and sensing from the edge to space networks.

I am a Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at The University of Hong Kong. My research interests include Space AI, Edge Intelligence, Over-the-Air Computation, and Quantum Receivers.

I received my Ph.D. from HKU in 2025 under the supervision of Prof. Kaibin Huang, and my M.Eng. (2021) and B.Eng. (2018) from Xidian University, supervised by Prof. Min Sheng.
